## GTA-Watch v2.1.1 — fixes the app crashing when the watch connects

### Phone app
- **Fixed: the app crashed every time the watch connected.** Android rejects any single Bluetooth write over 512 bytes, and on phones that negotiate a large packet size (MTU 517, e.g. Galaxy Z Fold2) the app built 514-byte packets, so the first map transfer crashed it. Packets are now capped at 512 bytes, and a rejected write can no longer take the app down.
- Found and verified on a Galaxy Z Fold2 (Android 17): connects at MTU 517, streams the map, terrain and live GPS to the watch with the phone asleep, no crashes.

### Watch
- When you're standing on a saved place, its blip no longer covers your arrow.

### Updating
- **App**: install `GTA-Watch.apk` over the previous version (settings and places are kept).
- **Watch**: in the app, open **Watch** or **Settings → Updates** and tap **Update watch to 2.1.1** (over Bluetooth, about a minute, keep the phone close), or flash `GTA-Watch-v2.1.1-app-0x10000.bin` at `0x10000` over USB.

Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
