## GTA-Watch v2.1.0 — terrain, charging, settings, and updates over the air

### Watch
- **Terrain on the minimap**: hills are shaded from real elevation data (AWS Terrain Tiles), kept subtle like GTA V's radar. Toggle in quick settings or the app.
- **Charging screen** when you plug in: percentage, a "breathing" charge ring, and time to full.
- **Low battery**: warnings at 20 / 10 / 5 %, and **power saver** below 15 % (dimmer screen, shorter timeout, lower CPU speed with the screen off).
- **Raise to wake**, smooth fade-in when the screen wakes, GTA-style loading screen at boot.
- **Radar zoom** setting (close / normal / far); it still zooms out automatically when driving fast.
- Watch face choice (digital / analog) is remembered; quick settings are now saved across restarts.
- Turn card shows the time while navigating; map blips no longer hide under it.
- **Firmware updates over Bluetooth** from the phone app (about a minute, with a progress screen; the image is verified before the watch switches to it).

### Phone app
- **Galaxy-Wearable-style pairing** through Android's companion device system: the link keeps running in the background and the app is woken when the watch comes into range.
- **Settings tab**: map style, terrain, radar zoom, watch face, raise to wake, spoken turn directions, fuel economy + gas price, charge / low-battery alerts, permissions, updates.
- **GTA Atlas map style** alongside the dark GTA Road pause map, both with terrain shading, plus a GTA-style **legend** on the map.
- **Watch battery card** with a 24-hour charge chart; notifications when the watch is charged or low.
- **Updates from this Releases page**: the app updates itself and the watch.
- **Spoken turn directions** (optional).
- **Crash fix**: the background service no longer crashes when Android restarts it while the app is closed (Android 14 location-service restriction). Any future crash is saved and shown in the Watch tab with a "Copy report" button.

### Tested
Watch firmware was tested on the watch over USB: terrain rendering, settings, the update path (a 1.3 MB image sent through the same message path the app uses, verified and booted), quick settings, the watch face. The phone app builds cleanly but **has not been run on a phone yet** — companion pairing, the in-app updater and Bluetooth firmware updates need a first real-device test. If anything misbehaves, the crash report in the Watch tab shows exactly what happened.

### Install
- **App**: download `GTA-Watch.apk` and install it over v2.0.0 (same signing key, your places and settings are kept). After installing, open the Watch tab and tap **Pair again** once to register the watch with Android's companion system.
- **Watch**: flash `GTA-Watch-v2.1.0-merged-0x0.bin` at `0x0` (web flasher or esptool, same steps as v2.0.0), or `GTA-Watch-v2.1.0-app-0x10000.bin` at `0x10000` over an existing v2 install. From v2.1.0 on, the app can update the watch over Bluetooth.

```bash
esptool --chip esp32s3 --port COM5 --baud 921600 write-flash 0x10000 GTA-Watch-v2.1.0-app-0x10000.bin
```

Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
